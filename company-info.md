# TServe Labs

**Software for the tennis community — and beyond.**

TServe Labs is a software consulting company founded by Shaunak Kashyap. We design, build, and ship production-quality software for startups, with a particular focus on the tennis community and the sports technology space.

---

## What We Do

We take projects from idea to deployed product. Depending on what the client needs, that means writing software, standing up infrastructure, or advising on technical architecture — often all three.

**Software Development**
End-to-end development of web applications, APIs, and platform services. We write clean, maintainable code and hand it off in a state the client can own and extend.

**Technical Consulting**
Architecture reviews, technology selection, and engineering strategy for founders and technical leaders who want an experienced outside perspective before committing to a direction.

**DevOps & Infrastructure**
Cloud setup, CI/CD pipelines, deployment automation, and reliability engineering. We get software running in production and make sure it stays there.

---

## Who We Work With

TServe Labs works primarily with early-stage startups — teams that need to move fast, make good technical decisions, and ship something real. We take on focused projects with a clear scope and outcome.

---

## How We Engage

Every engagement is project-based with a defined scope and deliverable. We do not do open-ended retainers or time-and-materials work. This keeps incentives aligned: we are motivated to deliver, not to bill hours.

A typical project produces:

- A working, deployed codebase
- Architecture and design documentation
- A smooth handoff so the client's team can take ownership
- An option for ongoing support after delivery

---

## Founder

**Shaunak Kashyap** founded TServe Labs to combine deep software engineering experience with a passion for the tennis community. Shaunak brings hands-on expertise across the full stack — from backend systems and cloud infrastructure to product thinking and technical leadership.
