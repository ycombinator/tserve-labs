iOS App

Your USTA NorCal league,
ready for match day.

Search the league, follow every team, compare TennisRecord and UTR ratings, plan a lineup, and see where the season is headed — all from one iPhone app.

Download on the App Store

Explore the app

Your season starts here

Open Net Results to your player profile, ratings, record, and teams—everything worth checking before the group chat starts asking questions.

  • Current NTRP, TennisRecord, and UTR ratings
  • Team records and next match details at a glance
  • Every current, upcoming, and past team in one place

All your teams, one view

Sign in with USTA NorCal to see every current, upcoming, and past team. Visit any player profile to browse their teams, too.

  • Win–loss record at a glance
  • Next match date and opponent shown on each card
  • TennisRecord, UTR-S, and UTR-D ratings on your profile
  • Export timed upcoming matches to your calendar

Plan a lineup

Toggle player availability locally, then compare an Equal Play proposal with a Strongest Lines proposal for the same match.

  • Available to signed-in players on that team and seeded from team availability
  • League format, gender, and rating limits respected
  • Recent partner records help inform doubles pairings

See where the flight is headed

Compare the actual standings with a projected end-of-season table and see each team’s likely movement in rank.

  • Current rank and record
  • Projected rank and record
  • Confidence and up, down, or unchanged movement

Know the roster before match day

See every player, rating, eligibility indicator, and record. Expand a player when you want their recent match history.

  • Sort by name, TennisRecord, or UTR
  • View an opponent’s roster before match day
  • Open a player’s recent results without losing your place

Find the matches that matter

Filter any player’s history by doubles partner, individual opponent, or the exact opposing doubles pair—and combine filters when the scouting gets serious.

  • Search partners, opponents, and opponent doubles pairs by name
  • Combine “With” and “Against” filters
  • See filtered match totals and win–loss records

Find a player or team in seconds

Search across USTA NorCal players and teams, then open a profile or team page directly from the result.

  • Player and team results together
  • Location and rating at a glance
  • Open a result directly into its profile

Never miss a match day detail

Upcoming matches show the time, location, captain notes, and your availability — all in one expanded row.

  • Match notes, such as gate codes or the water situation
  • Mark Yes, No, Maybe, or Call Last
  • Open the match location in your maps app

Every score, every line

Tap any result to see the full scorecard — who played each line, their opponents, the score, and available ratings.

  • Line-by-line match results
  • TennisRecord or UTR ratings shown for all players on each line
  • Captains can enter and verify scores after match day

Share the lineup in a tap

When a captain publishes the lineup, players can send it to opponents without the paperwork.

  • Review singles and doubles assignments before sharing
  • Send directly to a nearby Net Results user
  • Send a formatted lineup through Messages

See the player behind the rating

Expand a roster entry to review recent results and the season record that sits behind the roster view.

  • Recent opponent and partner history
  • Individual win–loss record
  • Ratings shown in the context of match results

Every detail for every team

Open a team for its schedule, record, roster, standings, and scorecards.

  • Current record and full season schedule
  • Upcoming and completed matches in one view
  • One tap from a team to its roster or standings

Keep account access under your control

Manage your USTA NorCal and UTR sessions, ratings, cached data, and protected Face ID sign-in from one clear Settings screen.

  • Turn Face ID off for either account at any time
  • Sign out of USTA and UTR separately
  • Choose which ratings appear throughout the app

Sign in to USTA NorCal securely

Connect your league account in a focused sign-in screen, then let Face ID handle the repeat visits. Typing passwords was never the fun part of tennis.

  • Protected credential storage on your device
  • Face ID for quick return access
  • Clear controls for saving or removing sign-in details

Bring your UTR ratings along

Connect UTR Sports to add singles and doubles ratings throughout Net Results, with the same optional Face ID protection for saved credentials.

  • UTR singles and doubles ratings across profiles and rosters
  • Face ID-protected return sign-in
  • Separate control from your USTA NorCal session

Know the courts before you arrive

Rate match venues and compare notes from players who have competed there—because sharing is caring, especially for court reconnaissance.

  • Score water, restrooms, lighting, courts, and food separately
  • See the venue’s overall rating at a glance
  • Share your full name or initials with each review

Report a problem without losing the plot

Send feedback from any screen with the useful technical context already attached, because “something went wrong somewhere” only gets us so far.

  • Include the current screen, team, and relevant app state
  • Attach a screenshot and recent network diagnostics
  • Review exactly what will be shared before sending
